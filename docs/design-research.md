## Original visual system
NOIRÉA is a fictional single-malt house at the fictional Loch Veyr. Its four-point mark represents land, water, oak and time. An architectural glass decanter, black mineral cap, understated dark label and amber liquid are the central visual character. Brand vocabulary: expression, quiet character, stillness, patient craft, the silent years.

Ink-black foundation, graphite surfaces, cream lettering, champagne accents, authentic amber from photography. Cormorant Garamond display paired with restrained Manrope body. Thin rules, sharp-corner outlined CTAs, asymmetric editorial composition, large empty spaces. No purchase claims, prices, real provenance or real products.

## Motion and performance
Generated original photographic media supplies realistic glass and liquid instead of an expensive unconvincing procedural model. GSAP transforms provide a 2.5D perspective/inspection experience, not a literal 360-degree rendering. Slow glass-light movement, parallax, scroll rotation and text reveals. No frame-sequence downloads, scroll hijacking, autoplay audio or perpetual full-screen graphics. All critical content remains readable without animation. Responsive WebP images, eager hero only, lazy editorial media. Reduced motion disables parallax, custom cursor, intro delay and decorative motion. Mobile maintains natural document flow and simplified transforms.
